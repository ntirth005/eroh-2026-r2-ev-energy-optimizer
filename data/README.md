# Data

Raw data files are **not** committed (size and licensing). Download them with the scripts in `../scripts/`:

| File | How it is obtained | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| `iex_dam_5yr_hourly.csv` | `python scripts/download_iex_dam.py` (Playwright drives the IEX India *Day-Ahead Market → Market Snapshot* page, Interval = Hourly, custom date range, 30-day chunks, Export → Excel; chunks land in `data/raw/iex_chunks/` and are merged) | Columns: `Date` (dd-mm-yyyy), `Hour` (1–24), Purchase Bid, Sell Bid, MCV, Final Scheduled Volume, `MCP (Rs/MWh) *`, Weighted MCP. 23 Nov 2021 – 22 Nov 2026 (43,104 rows). A 30-day gap (6 Apr – 5 May 2025) is filled automatically by the loader. |
| `era5_<DEL,MUM,BLR,HYD,MAA>.csv` | `python scripts/download_era5_weather.py` (Open-Meteo archive API, ERA5 reanalysis) | Hourly, local time (Asia/Kolkata), 22 Nov 2021 – 20 Nov 2026: `temperature_2m`, `cloud_cover`, `wind_speed_10m`, `wind_speed_100m` (km/h), `shortwave_radiation` (W/m²). Attribution: Copernicus Climate Change Service (ERA5), Open-Meteo (CC BY 4.0). |

Capacity factors are computed inside `src/datagen.py` from the weather:
- **Solar** (PVWatts-style): `cf = ghi/1000 · (1 + γ·(T_cell − 25)) · (1 − 0.14)`, `T_cell = T + 0.03·ghi`, `γ = −0.004`
- **Wind**: standard 3 MW onshore turbine curve at 100 m (cut-in 3, rated 12, cut-out 25 m/s) applied to `wind_speed_100m / 3.6`

Generated by the pipeline (git-ignored): `deg.json`, `calib.json`, `forecasts.pkl`, `runs/`.

`DATA_MODE=synthetic` needs no files: weather, renewable output and the IEX-like price are all generated.
